About these counts

Counts since tracking begins. Visits count browsing sessions, not unique people; refreshing or opening another tab shares the same session. Browsing now counts browsers active within 90 seconds. Roombas come from apps that enable “Share roomba count” and checked in within five minutes. Download clicks do not measure finished downloads or installs. Tracking blockers may prevent counts.

More work, more mess.

Every message you send your agent is a delivery dropped into the room. Pizza boxes pile up, the couch gets grimy, the fridge grows something. The roomba whose agent is working vacuums as it goes. Drag the slider to watch a day go by.

  • Working vacuums the junk
  • Needs you waiting for your approval
  • Stuck spins its wheels in a corner
  • Done confetti, then naps at the dock

Everything your agents do, at a glance.

A room for every project

Each project gets its own room, generated from its folder, so it always looks the same: wallpaper, floor, rug, windows and furniture picked to feel lived in. Flip between them from the sign on the roof. The arrows blink red when another room needs you.

The Garage holds everyone

One room with every agent from every project, and a car they can climb onto. It's the busiest room you'll own.

A whiteboard with three sides

Turn the crank. TODO holds your agents and your own sticky notes. ISSUES and PRS come straight from the project's GitHub, with CI lights and review checks. Click the board to spin it.

It nags until you look

When an agent is stuck, waiting for approval, or done, a siren spins on the roof, the frame pulses and the roomba shouts. You get a desktop notification too. Snooze it, or deal with it.

Shoo it to recharge

Done with an agent? Shoo it and it drives into the recharge pod and beams out. When that agent starts working again, it beams right back in.

Nothing to set up.

Roombai reads the session logs your Codex and Claude Code agents already write, including supported sessions from hosts like t3code. Install it, open it, and your roombas are there.

  • Codex CLIand VS Code
  • Codex appthe desktop app
  • Claude CodeCLI and SDK
  • t3codeA host for your agents

It only watches. It never sends anything to your agents. Issues and PRs use your existing GitHub CLI login.
